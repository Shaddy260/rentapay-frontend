# General managers and Brand Ambassadors

## General managers

A general manager is a more senior, trusted management role a landlord can choose
to set up, intended for someone who effectively runs the day to day operational
side of the landlord's business, potentially across several properties at once.

The first time a general manager needs to use a sensitive tool, they set their own
four digit Operations PIN, separate entirely from the password they use to log in.
That PIN is asked for again before certain higher impact actions can be completed,
adding a deliberate, second confirmation step beyond simply being logged in. A
general manager can change their own PIN at any time from settings, as long as they
know the current one.

Because a general manager acts with a wide scope of trust, every sensitive action
they take is recorded in an activity log the landlord can review at any time, and,
for a defined set of actions, the landlord can revert what was done within a
limited window afterward. This gives a landlord a genuine safety net without
requiring them to approve every single action a trusted general manager takes.

## Brand Ambassadors

A Brand Ambassador is an independent representative who introduces new landlords to
RentaPay under a separate arrangement, earning a payout once an introduced landlord
meets qualification criteria, such as remaining an active, paying subscriber for a
defined period.

A Brand Ambassador has their own dashboard, entirely separate from a landlord's or
manager's, showing only the landlords they themselves have introduced, along with
each one's current qualification status and payout status. A Brand Ambassador
cannot see any operational data belonging to a landlord they introduced beyond what
is needed to confirm that landlord still qualifies, and cannot see any other Brand
Ambassador's own introductions.

Qualification criteria and payout amounts are set by RentaPay and may be updated
from time to time, with the current criteria always visible within a Brand
Ambassador's own dashboard.

## What a General Manager can see across properties

A General Manager account is designed for someone overseeing several landlords'
portfolios at once, such as a management company employee, rather than a single
property. Their access spans the specific landlord accounts they have been given
oversight of, and every meaningful action they take, such as viewing a landlord's
financial summary or making a change on their behalf, is written to an activity log
tied to their own account, distinct from the landlord's own activity, so it is always
clear who did what.

## How a Brand Ambassador is paid

A Brand Ambassador earns a commission tied to the landlords they successfully
introduce to RentaPay who go on to subscribe. Commission rules and payout cycles are
tracked centrally and applied consistently; a submitted payout request goes through a
qualification and review step before being marked complete, and the history of past
payout cycles, amounts, and their status remains visible to the Brand Ambassador
within their own portal so they can always see where a submission stands.

## Submitting a prospective landlord

A Brand Ambassador submits a prospective landlord's basic contact details, typically
a name, phone number, and general location, ahead of that landlord creating their own
account. This submission links the eventual signup back to the Brand Ambassador for
commission purposes, but it does not create an account on the prospective landlord's
behalf; the landlord still goes through their own signup and remains in full control
of their own account, data, and password from the moment it is created.
