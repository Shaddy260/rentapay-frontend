# Understand RentaPay

## The problem

Collecting rent across even a handful of units usually means chasing bank
slips, cross-checking M-Pesa messages against a spreadsheet, and hoping
nothing falls through the cracks when a caretaker forgets to note a cash
payment. Multiply that across several properties, a property manager, and a
caretaker on the ground, and reconciliation becomes a part-time job on its
own.

## What RentaPay does

RentaPay is a single system that landlords, property managers, caretakers,
and tenants all use to keep rent collection, records, and reporting in one
place:

- **Landlords** see every property, every unit, and every payment — paid,
  pending, or late — without waiting on anyone to compile a report.
- **Property managers and caretakers** record payments as they happen
  (including manual/cash payments) so the landlord's view stays current.
- **Tenants** get a clear way to pay, submit their payment code, and get
  a receipt once it's approved — useful proof if a payment is ever in
  question.

## Who it's for

Whether you manage one unit or a hundred, across one building or several,
RentaPay scales with you — you can add properties, units, and staff as your
portfolio grows, without changing how the system works.

## Trust, in short

RentaPay never mixes up whose money is whose: every payment is tied to a
specific tenant, unit, and property, and only the people you've explicitly
given access to — a manager or caretaker you've invited — can see or act on
your properties. See the Privacy Policy for how your data is handled.
