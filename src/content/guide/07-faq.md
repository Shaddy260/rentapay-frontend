# Frequently asked questions

**What if a tenant doesn't pay?**
The unit shows as overdue in your dashboard as soon as the due date passes,
so you know immediately rather than at month-end reconciliation. RentaPay
records and reports; how you follow up is up to you and your lease terms.

**Can I manage multiple properties from one account?**
Yes — there's no limit on properties or units, and you can invite different
managers or caretakers to different properties.

**Is my data secure?**
Only people you've explicitly invited can access your properties, and
access is scoped to what you've given them. See the Privacy Policy for
details on how data is stored and handled.

**How do payments get confirmed?**
A tenant pays and submits their payment code in the app; the landlord (or
their manager/caretaker) reviews and approves it, and everything updates
automatically from there. See "How payments flow" above for the full
picture, including where automatic confirmation is headed.

**How do I get paid out, and how often?**
Payouts follow the schedule set in your account. You can see collected,
pending, and paid-out totals at any time from your dashboard.

**What happens if I want to stop using RentaPay?**
See the Terms & Conditions and Refund Policy for details on account
closure and any applicable terms.

**Where can tenants get help?**
Every portal — including the logged-out screen — shows the support call
number(s), WhatsApp number(s), and email address you've configured, so
tenants always have a direct way to reach support.
