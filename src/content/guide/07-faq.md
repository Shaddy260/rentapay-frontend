# Frequently asked questions

**What if a tenant does not pay?**
The unit shows as overdue in your dashboard as soon as the due date passes, so you
know immediately rather than at month end reconciliation. RentaPay records and
reports what has happened; how you follow up is a matter between you and your
tenant, under your own tenancy agreement and applicable law.

**Can I manage multiple properties from one account?**
Yes. There is no limit on properties or units, and you can invite different
managers, caretakers, or general managers to different properties.

**Does RentaPay ever hold my rent money?**
No. Rent is paid directly from tenant to landlord by M Pesa, whether through
Paybill, Till, or Send Money. RentaPay never holds, takes custody of, or has
control over that money at any point. It only records what the tenant submitted and
what the landlord, manager, or caretaker confirmed. See the Terms of Service for
the full explanation of what RentaPay is and is not.

**Is my data secure?**
Only people you have explicitly invited can access your properties, and their
access is scoped to what you have given them. Passwords and Operations PINs are
stored as one way cryptographic hashes, never as plain text, and every request is
sent over an encrypted connection. See the Privacy Policy for full detail on how
data is stored and handled.

**How do payments get confirmed?**
A tenant pays and submits their payment code in the app; the landlord, or their
manager or caretaker, reviews and approves it, and everything updates automatically
from there. See "How payments flow" earlier in this guide for the full picture,
including how a landlord's own subscription payment can be confirmed automatically
instead.

**How is a security deposit handled?**
You record the amount a tenant paid at move in, kept completely separate from
their rent balance. When the tenancy ends, you record whether it was refunded in
full, partially refunded with a stated reason, or forfeited. RentaPay never holds
that money either; it is purely a record of what was agreed and what happened.

**What happens if I want to stop using RentaPay?**
See the Terms of Service for details on account suspension and termination, and the
Privacy Policy for how a data deletion request works.

**Where can tenants get help?**
Every portal, including the logged out screens, shows the support call number,
WhatsApp number, and email address configured for your account, plus in app chat,
so tenants always have a direct way to reach support.

**Can a tenant take their payment history to a new landlord?**
A tenant's aggregate rating, built from every landlord who has rated them, is tied
to their own email address, not to any one landlord's account, so it can follow
them to a new landlord on RentaPay entirely at the tenant's own choice, or be
shared through a link the tenant generates themselves.

**Can a general manager revert an action they took by mistake?**
For a defined set of sensitive actions, yes. A landlord can review a general
manager's activity log and revert certain actions within a limited window after
they were taken.

**What happens if a tenant cannot pay the full month at once?**
They can propose a payment plan directly from their portal, splitting the amount
into at least two installments with their own due dates, for the landlord to
approve or decline.

**What happens when a tenant gives notice to vacate?**
Their unit is marked as notice given immediately, and RentaPay reminds them
automatically as the move out date approaches. On that date, their account is
deactivated and the unit becomes vacant, ready to be re listed, while their
payment history and portable reputation record are preserved rather than deleted.

**Can a tenant change their mind after giving notice?**
Yes, as long as it is before the notice date itself. They should contact their
landlord, manager, or caretaker directly to have the notice revoked.

**Can I bill tenants for water or electricity through RentaPay?**
Yes, through utility submetering. See the dedicated section of this guide for how
shared and individual meters, readings, and billing work.


## How do I know if RentaPay is down or if it's just me?

Check the public status page, reachable without signing in. It reports on
seventeen individual parts of the platform separately, so you'll see by name
exactly which feature, if any, has an issue — not just a vague "something's wrong."

## Why does my session log me out after a week?

By default, signing in keeps you signed in for seven days before you're asked to
sign in again, as a security measure. Signing out manually, or changing your
password, ends a session immediately rather than waiting for that week to pass.

## Can I get a report or export from RentaPay?

Yes — annual reports, tax summaries, financial CSVs, and receipt bundles are all
available from within the app. Larger ones are generated in the background and
delivered as a time-limited download link, valid for one hour; smaller ones may be
generated immediately.

## What if a notification (email, SMS, WhatsApp) doesn't arrive?

Check the app itself first — the underlying record (a payment, a maintenance
update) is always accurate regardless of whether an accompanying notification
arrived. If a specific delivery channel is having a problem, it will show as an
issue on the status page.

## Can more than one person manage the same property?

Yes — a landlord can add property managers and caretakers to the same account, each
with their own login, and set what each one can see and do. Every action any of
them takes is attributed to their own account in the activity log, so it's always
clear who made a specific change even when several people manage the same
property.

## What happens to my data if I stop using RentaPay?

Your records remain stored according to our standard retention approach, described
in the Privacy Policy, rather than being deleted the moment you stop actively using
the app. If you want your account or data deleted outright, you can request that
directly through Help, and we'll handle it according to the deletion rights
described in the Privacy Policy.

## Is my rent payment held by RentaPay?

No. RentaPay is not a bank, an escrow agent, or a custodian of your money at any
point. An M-Pesa payment goes directly between payer and recipient via Safaricom;
RentaPay only records that the payment happened, it never holds, controls, or has
access to the funds themselves.

## Does RentaPay work if I have a weak internet connection?

RentaPay is built to be lightweight — most screens are just text and numbers, which
load quickly even on a slow connection. Photos are the heaviest thing the app
transfers; if uploading one is slow, that's usually your connection specifically
rather than the app itself, which you can confirm on the status page.
