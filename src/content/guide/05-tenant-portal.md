# The Tenant portal

![Tenant portal: rent balance, till number, Pay Rent button, and unit information](./images/real/tenant-portal.png)
*Real product screenshot — tenant name and balance shown are illustrative.*

Tenants use RentaPay to:

- **See exactly what's due**, including whether it's upcoming or overdue,
  and the till/account number to pay to.
- **Pay and submit their payment code** — after paying to the number
  shown, the tenant enters the confirmation code in the app, which the
  landlord or their manager/caretaker then reviews and approves.
- **View their full payment breakdown and history**, and access a receipt
  once a payment is approved.
- **Reach support** — call, WhatsApp, or email — directly from the portal
  if something isn't right. These contact details are kept current by the
  landlord's team, so tenants always have a working way to get help.

![Tenant sidebar menu: My Reputation, Documents, FAQs, Help/Complaints, Virtual Assistant, Settings](./images/real/tenant-sidebar-menu.png)
*Real product screenshot.*

Tenants also have access to their reputation history, documents, FAQs,
and a way to raise a complaint or reach a virtual assistant directly from
their own menu.
