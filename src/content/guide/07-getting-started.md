# Getting started

1. **Sign up** as a landlord with your basic contact details.
2. **Add your properties and units** — as many as you have, whenever you
   have them. There's no limit and no extra step for adding more later.
3. **Invite your managers and caretakers**, scoped to the specific
   properties they should have access to.
4. **Add your tenants**, either yourself or by having your manager/
   caretaker do it — each tenant gets their own portal access.
5. **Start collecting** — tenants can pay digitally, or your team can
   record manual payments, from day one.

Most landlords are set up and seeing their first payments within the same
day. There's no paperwork beyond your own account details and the
properties you want to add.
