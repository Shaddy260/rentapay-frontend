# How pricing works

RentaPay works on a **subscription**, not a per-transaction commission.
When a landlord signs up, they subscribe **per unit, per month** — the
price is set by RentaPay and may change over time depending on market
conditions.

## How subscribing works

- At sign-up, a landlord chooses how many units to subscribe and for how
  long (for example, 22 units for two months).
- The subscription covers those units for that period — collecting rent
  through RentaPay during that window doesn't cost anything extra per
  payment.
- **When a subscription expires**, the landlord gets a message prompting
  them to renew. For example, if a 22-unit, two-month subscription runs
  out, that landlord is notified to subscribe again to keep using
  RentaPay for those units.

> **Current subscription rates are confirmed during sign-up**, since they
> can move with the market. Reach out via the contact details on this
> page if you'd like current pricing before you sign up.

## What's included

- Unlimited tenant accounts for your subscribed units
- Manager and caretaker accounts with permission controls
- Payment records, receipts, and reporting
- Support via call, WhatsApp, and email
