# How pricing works

RentaPay works on a **subscription**, not a commission taken from each rent
payment. When a landlord signs up, they subscribe **per unit, per month**. The
price per unit is set by RentaPay and may change over time, and the current rate
is always shown within the app before a landlord confirms a purchase.

## How subscribing works

- At sign up, a landlord chooses how many units to subscribe and for how long, for
  example twenty two units for two months.
- The subscription covers those units for that period. Collecting and confirming
  rent through RentaPay during that window does not cost anything extra per
  payment, no matter how many payments come through.
- When a subscription is close to expiring, the landlord is notified within the app
  and by email, so renewal is never a surprise.
- If a subscription lapses without renewal, access to certain landlord side
  features, such as onboarding a new tenant or receiving the optional portfolio
  digest email, may be reduced until it is renewed. Every record already saved,
  tenants, payment history, deposits, and everything else, stays exactly where it
  was and is not deleted for a lapsed subscription alone.

## How a subscription is actually paid

A landlord has two ways to pay a subscription:

- **Automatic, through M Pesa STK push.** RentaPay itself triggers a prompt on the
  landlord's phone through Safaricom's Daraja API. The landlord enters their M Pesa
  PIN once, and the payment is confirmed automatically the moment Safaricom reports
  it back to RentaPay, with no manual review step needed on either side.
- **Manual, by sending payment directly.** A landlord can instead send payment to
  RentaPay's own Paybill or Till number outside the app, and then submit proof of
  that payment within RentaPay, the same way a tenant submits proof of a rent
  payment. This starts as a pending submission and is reviewed and confirmed by a
  RentaPay administrator, which typically takes a little longer than the automatic
  option since it involves a human check.

## Loyalty discounts

RentaPay may from time to time grant a landlord a discount on their subscription in
recognition of a long standing, continuously active account. Where a discount is
granted, it is shown within the app along with its size and its expiry date, and an
unused discount that is not applied before that date can lapse.

## What is included

- Unlimited tenant accounts for your subscribed units
- Manager, caretaker, and general manager accounts with permission controls
- Security deposit tracking, kept separate from the rent ledger
- Utility submetering for shared or individual water and electricity billing
- Payment records, receipts, and portfolio reporting, including an annual report
  and a data export
- Tenant ratings, portable reputation, disputes, and the community board
- Support through the in app chat, plus phone, WhatsApp, and email

## How the monthly rate is calculated

A landlord's subscription cost is the base rate per unit per month, multiplied by
however many units are on their account at the time of billing. Adding or removing
units changes what the next billing period costs; it does not retroactively change
what a period already paid for cost.

## Prepaying for longer periods

Paying for three, six, or twelve months at once, rather than one month at a time,
unlocks a discounted effective monthly rate - the longer the prepaid period, the
larger the discount. The exact rate and discount percentages in force at the moment
a subscription payment is calculated are the ones recorded against that payment
permanently, even if the standard rate changes afterward.

## What happens if a subscription lapses

If a subscription isn't renewed by its due date, the account enters a short grace
period during which existing data stays visible but some features may be limited.
If it still isn't renewed after that, the account moves into a locked state.
Renewing at any point, including after lapsing, restores full access immediately -
RentaPay does not delete a landlord's property, unit, tenant, or payment history
just because a subscription lapsed.
