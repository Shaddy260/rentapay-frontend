# Getting started

1. **Sign up** as a landlord with your basic contact details: full name, phone
   number, and email address.
2. **Add your properties and units**, as many as you have, whenever you have them.
   There is no limit and no extra step for adding more later. Each unit can have
   its own rent amount, due date, deposit requirement, and photos.
3. **Choose your subscription**, per unit and per month, and pay it either
   automatically by M Pesa STK push, confirmed the moment Safaricom reports it, or
   manually by sending payment to RentaPay's own Paybill or Till number and
   submitting proof for review.
4. **Invite your managers, caretakers, and, if you need one, a general manager**,
   scoped to the specific properties they should have access to. A general manager
   sets their own Operations PIN the first time they use a sensitive tool.
5. **Add your tenants**, either yourself or by having your manager or caretaker do
   it, or by turning on self onboarding so an invited tenant can complete their own
   details. Each tenant gets their own portal access, and you can record whether
   they paid a security deposit as part of onboarding.
6. **Set up utility submetering**, if your property has a shared or individual
   water or electricity meter you want to bill tenants for.
7. **Start collecting.** Tenants can pay by M Pesa and submit their code, or your
   team can record a manual or cash payment, from day one.

Most landlords are set up and seeing their first payments within the same day.
There is no paperwork beyond your own account details and the properties you want
to add.

## What happens the very first time you sign in

The first time a tenant, caretaker, or general manager signs in using credentials a
landlord or manager created for them, RentaPay treats that as a first-time sign in
and may prompt you to set your own password rather than continuing to use the
temporary one you were given. This is a one-time step; once you have set your own
password, future sign ins simply ask for it directly.

## Setting up a landlord account from scratch

A landlord creating their own account for the first time is asked for their name,
phone number, and email address, then chooses a password. From there, the natural
next steps are: add your first property, add its units with their monthly rent
amounts and due dates, and either invite each tenant by phone number and email so
they can set their own password, or generate first-time login credentials for a
tenant yourself if they are not comfortable doing that part on their own. None of
these steps has to happen in one sitting; a landlord can add one unit and one tenant,
confirm everything looks right, and add the rest over the following days.

## What a tenant needs before they can sign in

A tenant cannot create their own RentaPay account from nothing; an account is created
for them, either by a landlord, property manager, caretaker, or general manager
adding them directly, or by the tenant using an invitation link containing details
the landlord has already entered. Either way, by the time a tenant signs in for the
first time, their unit, rent amount, and due date are already recorded, so their
very first screen already shows accurate, useful information rather than an empty
dashboard.
